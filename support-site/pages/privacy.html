Privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

Saving a workout in VYRO creates a private training record. You choose whether to back it up, post it to your followers or contribute a route to a competition.

Who is responsible?

Xamse Shaqlane is responsible for VYRO’s processing of personal data. For privacy questions or requests, email vyrosupport@gmail.com.

Your account and profile

VYRO uses Supabase to manage sign-in and store your account identifier, email address and profile. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive an identifier and the information that provider shares, such as your email or Apple relay address. Other signed-in users can find your username, display name and profile picture. A private profile lets you approve follow requests.

GPS and workouts

With location access, the app can show your position and record a route during a GPS workout. It uses location samples and timestamps to calculate distance, pace or speed. If you enable background recording and allow the necessary access in iOS, recording can continue while the phone is locked. You can change these permissions in iPhone Settings.

Your training log on the device can include the sport, date, active time, distance, route, effort and notes. A paired Apple Watch can record a workout and, with your permission, read heart rate through Apple Health and transfer workout data to your iPhone. If you enable the Lock Screen display, someone looking at your phone may see workout details.

Backups and sharing

Private cloud backup is optional. VYRO encrypts the workout on your device before sending it to Supabase. The service stores the encrypted record and identifiers needed to restore it. Keep your recovery code safe. Support cannot recover your private backup if you lose both the code and the device.

Publishing makes a separate post available to the audience you confirm. It can contain your name, workout statistics and a map. Private notes and effort stay out of posts. For new shared workout maps, VYRO hides GPS points within 200 metres of the start and finish, including later passes. Visible landmarks and workout totals can still reveal places you visited. Check the preview before sharing.

Contributing a route or joining a club zone competition is a separate choice. Other participants can see shared route geometry, zone results and the profile associated with a result. Follows, requests, likes, comments and club memberships are stored to provide the social features you use.

Pictures and notifications

If you choose a profile or place photo, VYRO uploads the selected image to Supabase Storage so people who have access can see it. A picture can reveal a person or location even when a map is hidden. If you enable push notifications, an Expo push token is associated with your account and device. Expo and Apple deliver the selected alerts. Previews may be visible on your Lock Screen.

Why we use this information

We process account and service data to provide the features you request under our agreement with you. Optional location and health access depends on your permission; you can withdraw it in device settings. We use reports and technical records for our legitimate interest in preventing abuse, securing accounts and resolving problems. If you email support, we use your address and message to answer your request.

Services involved

Supabase hosts authentication, database records and pictures. Apple provides iPhone maps, address lookups and optional Apple sign-in; Google provides optional Google sign-in. The web map uses Mapbox for map display and address search. Map services receive requests needed to display the area or search you select. Support email is handled through Google. Service providers may process information outside your country. Contact us for details about where your data is processed and applicable safeguards.

Retention and deletion

Local workouts remain until you remove them. Withdrawing a post removes the shared version. Deleting a local workout does not delete a cloud backup. To remove backups, turn backup off on your devices and use the cloud deletion option in Data and backup.

To delete your account, go to You → Settings → Account and security → Delete account. Deletion removes the account, associated posts, comments, photos, routes, clubs you own and private backups. Remove exports or copies on other devices separately. Recipients may already have saved a copy. Abuse reports are handled manually and have no automatic expiry.

Your rights

You can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability where applicable, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Withdrawing consent applies to future processing. Email vyrosupport@gmail.com to make a request. You may also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority or your local supervisory authority.
