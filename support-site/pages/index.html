VYRO support

Get help with your account, a recorded workout or a post.

Email VYRO support

You can also write to vyrosupport@gmail.com in English or Norwegian. If something went wrong, tell us which screen you were on and what happened. Keep passwords, recovery codes and private GPS tracks out of your message.

Common questions

How do I sign back in?

Choose the method you used when you registered. For Sign in with Apple, use the same Apple Account, including if you chose Hide My Email. For an email and password account, tap Forgot password? on the sign-in screen.

Where is my workout?

Open You → Activities on the device that recorded it. To restore an encrypted backup on a new phone, sign in to the same VYRO account and open Settings → Data and backup. You’ll need the recovery code you saved when you enabled backup.

Who can see my route?

Saving a workout keeps it in your private log. If you publish a post, check its audience and map preview first. To withdraw it, open your post on Home. You can keep the original workout in your log.

How do I delete my account?

Open You → Settings → Account and security → Delete account. Sign in again if asked, type the confirmation word shown on screen and confirm. Export anything you want to keep first. If you can’t access the account, email us for help.

How do I report abuse or request my data?

Use the report or block option in VYRO, or email support with the username and what happened. For a privacy request, tell us what you need, such as access, correction or deletion. We may ask you to verify account ownership.
